Recent Devlogs & Changelog

CHANGELOG • v2.5 October 29, 2026 • By @veenkes

Custom Video Player for Posts Mode + Click-to-Play Grid Optimization

Two big fixes went live tonight based on direct feedback. First, in Grid mode, video tiles were previously preloading metadata/streams in the background on scroll, which wasted bandwidth if you just wanted to browse thumbnails. I switched all Grid video tiles to preload="none" so they sit quietly as lightweight poster thumbnails until you explicitly click the Play button on a tile.

Second, I finished porting the full custom HTML5 player bar over to Posts mode (/posts/...). As you can see in the screenshot above, every video in Posts view now has a unified dark glass control bar with Play/Pause, a smooth timeline scrubber, live duration counter, audio mute + volume slider, playback speed toggle (1x / 1.25x / 1.5x / 2x), and fullscreen.

Test Video Player in Posts Mode →
UI & CSS FIXES • v2.4 October 28, 2026 • By @veenkes

Fixed Drawer Layering, Card Score Badges & Narrower 1160px Layout

Tracked down and fixed a nasty CSS stacking context bug where the toolbar buttons (Posts | Grid, S | M | L, and the board dropdown) had a higher z-index than the slide-out navigation menu and right-side filter drawer. Rebuilt the entire z-index hierarchy so drawers and modals always sit cleanly above the feed controls.

Also fixed a layout glitch on boards like Yande.re where posts with 0 score rendered an empty star circle in the top-right corner of the card — cards with 0 score now display their clean file format badge (JPG, PNG, MP4) instead. Finally, narrowed the main page container from 1360px down to 1160px across all routes so the grid feels much tighter on widescreen monitors.

Welcome to Veenkes Studio — Why I Built R34Hub & How Pro Keys Work
